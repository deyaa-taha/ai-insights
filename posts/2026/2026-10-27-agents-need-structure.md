Everyone starts with one big model and one long prompt. It works in the demo. It breaks in production.

What made the difference for me when designing an agentic SDLC platform:

→ An orchestrator that runs a deterministic workflow
→ Specialized agents with one job each
→ Markdown artifacts as the source of truth, not chat history
→ Quality gates, with a human approving the critical ones
→ An AI Gateway and FinOps from day one, not after the first invoice

Smarter models help. Structure is what makes them reliable.
